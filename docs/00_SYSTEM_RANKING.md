## Why `pow-physical` is the next full build

It joins multiple hard economic layers immediately:

`device → component → failure → repair procedure → part availability → parts price → resale value → skill`

It serves:
- repair intelligence
- flipping economics
- parts procurement
- spare-part scarcity
- obsolescence
- dropship/commerce research
- robot/edge-AI future components
- future Muse actions without becoming Muse

The initial appliance wedge is unusually data-rich.

## Why `pow-uk` is second

It supplies the human-capacity side of the same constraint graph:

`task → skill → occupation → qualification → training flow → local labour supply → local demand`

This is the bridge from:
“this hardware is failing”
to:
“capacity to fix/install it is scarce.”

## Why `pow-grid` is third

Power is a universal physical input to:
- compute
- datacentres
- EVs
- heat pumps
- robotics
- factories
- mining

The UK has unusually strong public power-system data.

## Why a tiny `pow-capital` collector starts immediately

Deep order-book state is intrinsically ephemeral.

Even if the causal model is not ready, begin collecting a small, rights-safe internal research universe now so future event studies have pre-event history.

Do not delay the recorder until the graph is sophisticated.

## Why `pow-frontier` stays thin initially

Its job is not to become “tech news.”

It turns credible technical changes into structured hypotheses:

`breakthrough → constraint relaxed → possible new constraint → resources affected → predictions → kill conditions`

The expensive thing is not collecting papers; it is testing those hypotheses against the other gardens.
